## Function Description

- Operator function: generates a random number tensor that follows the uniform distribution based on the Philox4x32-10 pseudo-random number generation algorithm. The random values are in the range [from, to), and the interpretation of the bounds and upper-bound wrap follows the values after conversion to the output data type.

- Computation formula:

  Let $T$ be the output data type (FLOAT32, FLOAT16, or BFLOAT16), and $from$ and $to$ be DOUBLE scalars. They are first converted to FLOAT32 in the tiling stage, then converted to $T$ to obtain the bounds $a$ and $b$; the difference $d$ is also stored as $T$:

  $$
  a = T(\text{FLOAT32}(from)), \quad b = T(\text{FLOAT32}(to)), \quad d = T(b - a)
  $$

  Here, $x_i$ is the raw uint32 random number generated by the Philox4x32-10 algorithm, and $u_i$ and the candidate value $v_i$ are computed as follows:

  $$
  u_i = \text{FLOAT32}\left(\text{FLOAT32}(x_i) \times 2^{-32} + 2^{-33}\right), \quad
  v_i = T\left(\text{FLOAT32}(u_i) \times \text{FLOAT32}(d) + \text{FLOAT32}(a)\right)
  $$

  The candidate value is first converted back to the output data type and then compared with the upper bound $b$ using `==`: if equal, it is replaced with the lower bound $a$; otherwise $v_i$ is kept:

  $$
  y_i = \begin{cases}
  a, & v_i = b \\
  v_i, & v_i \neq b
  \end{cases}
  $$

  The normalization of $u_i$ is the same as that of the competitor curand_uniform, i.e., $u = x \times 2^{-32} + 2^{-33}$ (computed in FLOAT32). Due to FLOAT32 rounding, $u_i$ may reach 1 in FLOAT32, and the candidate value in FLOAT16/BFLOAT16 may also equal the upper bound after conversion to the output type; when the candidate value is exactly equal to the upper bound $b$, the upper-bound wrap above is triggered. Therefore, $[from, to)$ should be interpreted based on the converted bounds: when $a < b$, $y_i \in [a, b)$ usually holds, but due to floating-point rounding, when $u_i$ is close to 1 the candidate value may be slightly greater than $b$ and not equal to $b$ (no wrap), in which case $y_i$ may slightly exceed $b$; the lower bound $a$ always holds. When $a = b$ (a degenerate case where $from$ and $to$ coincide after conversion to the output type), $y_i$ is always $a$. The above computation does not change the generation rule of the Philox random sequence and applies only to this operator and its supported products.

## Constraint Description

- offset must be a multiple of 4.
- The output tensor supports 0 to 8 dimensions.
- from must be less than or equal to to, and to - from cannot exceed the representation range of the output data type.
